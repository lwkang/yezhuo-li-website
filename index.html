Yezhuo Li

Researcher Assistant Professor · School of Cybersecurity
Old Dominion University

I work on computational statistics and uncertainty quantification for reliable decision-making in adaptive systems. My research combines Bayesian optimization, sequential experimental design, and uncertainty estimation with applications spanning composite-material manufacturing, autonomous and cyber-physical systems, and federated learning security. I completed my Ph.D. in Mathematical Science at Clemson University in 2025.

Research

Computational Statistics and Uncertainty Quantification for Reliable Decision-Making in Adaptive Systems.

Uncertainty Quantification & Bayesian Optimization

Constrained Bayesian optimization and sequential design methods for optimal decision-making under uncertainty, with applications to composite-material cure process optimization and reduction of warpage in injection molding. Work sponsored by the Artificially Intelligent Manufacturing Paradigm for Composites, a DOE Energy Frontier Research Center.

Security & Reliability of Cyber-Physical Systems

Statistical and machine-learning methods for resilience and anomaly detection in adaptive systems, including autonomous vehicle safety analysis, cyber-physical resilience planning for autonomous port networks, budgeted validation against suspicious updates in federated learning, and graph-based anomaly detection in water cyber-physical systems.
